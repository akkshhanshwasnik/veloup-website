Common worries

Worried About Risk?

We understand AI feels risky. That's why we start with just 14 days of lead handling. You see the results before committing more. No pressure, no obligations.

Worried About Cost?

We don't show prices. A custom quote follows your free call. We offer small paid pilots that are credited if you continue with us.

Worried About Staff Replacement?

We don't replace staff. We handle repeat follow-ups so your team can focus on site visits and customers.

Worried About Data Safety?

Your customer details stay with your business. We don't store or use your data for other purposes.

What if a customer needs a person?

The assistant hands the conversation to your team when a question needs a human, and tells the customer someone will reply.

Will it sound like a robot?

No. We write the replies with you, in your words and tone, and you approve them before anything goes live.
