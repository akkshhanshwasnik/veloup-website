The enquiry you miss is the customer you lose

Most small businesses don't lose customers because of price. They lose them because nobody replied. The phone rings while you're with a patient, showing a flat or serving a table. A WhatsApp message arrives at 9 pm and gets answered the next afternoon. By then the customer has called someone else.

VeloUp's WhatsApp and missed-call follow-up closes that gap. An AI assistant replies the moment an enquiry comes in, asks what the customer needs, offers your real free slots and books the next step: an appointment, a site visit, a table or a demo class. If the customer goes quiet, it follows up. Your team gets every enquiry in one place, with what the customer wants already noted.

What a missed call looks like with VeloUp

A patient calls a clinic after hours. Nobody picks up. Instead of a lost appointment, this happens:

  1. The assistant sends a WhatsApp message to the caller within seconds.
  2. It asks what they need and offers the doctor's real free slots.
  3. The patient picks a time. The booking is confirmed in the chat.
  4. The next morning, a reminder goes out with an option to reschedule.

The same pattern works for a property enquiry (budget, area, site-visit time), a restaurant booking (party size, time, occasion) or a coaching admission (course, batch, demo class). Want to try a full conversation? Open our salon demo and book an appointment yourself.

Questions owners ask

Will it sound like a robot?

No. We write the replies with you, in your words and tone, and you approve them before anything goes live.

What if a customer needs a person?

The assistant hands the conversation to your team when a question needs a human, and tells the customer someone will reply.

Do I need technical knowledge?

No. You share your common questions, working hours and free slots. We do the setup and testing.

Is my customers' data safe?

Your customer details stay with your business. We don't store or use your data for other purposes. See our privacy policy.

How much does it cost?

We don't show prices on the website. You get a custom quote after a free 15-minute call. We offer small paid pilots that are credited toward future services if you continue with us.

What if I don't want to continue after 14 days?

You can stop. After 14 days we review together what happened, and you decide to continue, change or stop.
