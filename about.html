how it started —

Our Story

VeloUp started with a pattern we couldn't unsee. The phone rings during the lunch rush. A WhatsApp message arrives after closing. By the time someone replies, the customer has already booked somewhere else.

The businesses weren't doing anything wrong. They were busy doing the actual work. What they lacked wasn't effort; it was a free minute at the exact moment a customer reached out.

So we built VeloUp: friendly AI assistants that reply in seconds, ask the right questions and book the next step. We set them up and look after them, so owners never have to learn new software. We're starting in Pune, with businesses we can meet in person.
